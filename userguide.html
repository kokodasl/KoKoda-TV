KoKoda Radio

KoKoda Radio controls the music stream for your Second Life parcel, rather than shared media on a screen.

Getting started

  1. Rez the radio on your parcel with scripts enabled. The object must be owned by the land owner or deeded to the land-owning group as appropriate. Set up administrator access before deeding, following the controller instructions.
  2. Click the radio and choose Radio Stations, a genre, then a station. Use Next, Prev and Back to navigate.
  3. Enable streaming music in your viewer and adjust the Music volume.

Power and access

Choosing a station starts parcel music and turns the power symbol turquoise. Turn Off clears the parcel music URL and turns the symbol red. Turn On restores the last selected station; choose a station first if none has been saved.

The Access menu lets the administrator choose Owner Only, Group Only (matching active group), or Public. These settings control who can operate the radio, not who can hear parcel music.

Adding your own stations

  1. Edit the radio and open its Contents tab.
  2. Open the editable notecard named My Stations.
  3. Add one station per line using the format below. Save the notecard.
  4. Click the radio, select My Stations, then choose your genre and station.
Genre|Station Name|URL
Rock|Classic Rock|https://example.com/rock
Pop|My Pop Station|https://example.com/pop

These are examples only. Replace the URLs with actual direct audio streams. Use consistent genre names, no extra pipe characters, and one entry per line. The radio groups entries automatically. Your custom stations stay separate from the official online catalogue.

Automatic updates

The official station catalogue is fetched online when browsing. New genres and stations become available without replacing the radio; a currently playing stream is not automatically changed.

Troubleshooting

No sound or the station does not change

Check streaming music is enabled, Music volume is audible, and you are on the correct parcel. Verify the radio has parcel ownership permissions. Try another station.

My custom station is missing or silent

Check the notecard name, save changes, and confirm each line has three pipe-separated fields. Use a direct compatible audio stream, not a website or embedded player.

Menu or catalogue will not load

Check script permissions and access settings. Wait briefly and reopen the menu. The official catalogue requires an internet connection.

Music changes unexpectedly

Other authorized radios or parcel controls can change the same parcel music URL. Check for competing controllers.

Report a broken station

Try another official station first. If only one fails, open the Radio Station Directory, find the station and click Report broken station. Add optional details and submit. For personal stations, check with the stream provider.

Getting started

  1. Rez your KES-powered device on land where you have permission to rez objects and run scripts.
  2. Click the device frame or designated control surface to open the control menu. If the screen is already playing media, click the control surface rather than the video.
  3. Choose Movies, TV Shows or YouTube to begin.

On KoKoda TV, the logo glows turquoise when media is active and red when the TV is off. KoKoda TV uses shared media; each viewer must allow media playback.

Using the device menu

Movies

Select Movies, choose a genre, then select a numbered title from the list. Use Next and Prev to browse pages and Back to return to the previous menu.

TV shows

Select TV Shows, choose a series, select a season, then choose an episode. Use the numbered buttons corresponding to the displayed entries.

YouTube

Select YouTube, paste a video URL into the text box and submit it. Some videos may not allow embedded playback.

Other controls

TV Guide displays the entertainment guide on the television and offers to open it in your browser. Help opens this manual in your browser without changing the current screen. Turn Off stops media and restores the off screen. Cancel closes the menu.

Access settings

The owner can select Access to choose who controls the device.

  • Owner: Only the owner.
  • Group: Owner and avatars whose active group matches the device object's group.
  • Public: Anyone.

These settings control the menu, not who can see shared media. The controller defaults to Owner after a script reset.

Media and sound

  1. Enable shared media in your Second Life viewer. In Firestorm, check Preferences → Sound & Media and the media settings; labels may vary by version.
  2. Use the viewer's media play control or click the screen if autoplay is blocked.
  3. Check your viewer's media volume and computer audio output.

Each viewer loads media independently, so playback may not be perfectly synchronized. Some providers restrict embedding or require interaction before playing audio.

Entertainment Guide

Browse the Entertainment Guide to discover movies and series, search by title or cast, and filter by genre. Select the actual movie or episode using your KES-powered device.

The catalogue is retrieved online, so additions and removals normally do not require a controller update.

Report broken content

  1. Open the Entertainment Guide and select the affected title.
  2. For a series, enter the season and episode if known.
  3. Add optional details about the problem, then select Report broken content.

Reports are sent to catalogue support. Confirmation may take a minute; you can close the popup and continue browsing, but keep the page open until the request finishes. Please avoid duplicate reports and do not include personal information.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

The menu does not open

Click the device control surface, check access permissions and ensure scripts are allowed on the parcel. If a catalogue is loading, wait and try again.

The screen is blank or there is no sound

Check shared media settings, media volume and playback controls. Try another title to distinguish viewer issues from broken content.

A movie or episode will not play

Try another title. If only one title fails, report it through the Entertainment Guide. External media may be removed or become unavailable.

The catalogue does not load

Wait briefly and reopen the menu. KES requires access to its online catalogue. If the issue persists, contact KoKoda support.

Do I need to update my device when new titles are added?

Normally no. KES retrieves catalogue pages online. Controller updates may still be needed for new features.

Why is my friend watching a different point in the video?

Shared media playback is handled by individual viewers and is not guaranteed to be synchronized.
